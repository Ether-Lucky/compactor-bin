# Product: Compactor Bin Simulator

Inferred from SPEC.md (the agreed spec); no separate interview was run.

- **What it is:** a browser simulator of a student-built automatic plastic-bottle compactor bin (Arduino UNO, HC-SR04 sensors, MG996R lid servo, BTS7960 driver, two 12V 1500N linear actuators).
- **Who uses it:** a high-school / college student group building the bin, their mentor at group meetings, and a teacher/panel watching a presentation on a projector.
- **Use scene:** laptops at home or in class, sometimes offline; one shared screen or projector during presentation. Mixed lighting, so both light and dark themes follow the system.
- **Mode:** Operate. Success = the visitor understands cause and effect (sensor → decision → lid → crush → power) and can test design choices quickly.
- **Tone:** a precise workshop test bench. Real units, honest labels for estimates, plain English. No decoration that isn't part of the machine.
- **Constraints:** runs by double-clicking index.html on any computer, offline; no build step; no personal information in the repo.
